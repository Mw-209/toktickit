## 2. Unresolved Decisions & Open Inquiries

1. **Attachment Upload in Create Ticket vs Ticket Detail:**
   - Handout permits uploading attachments during initial ticket creation and adding more on Ticket Detail.
   - *Strategy:* Multipart form-data or 2-step creation (create ticket record, then upload attachments in transaction).
2. **Soft-removal Reason Requirement:**
   - Handout requires reason metadata. We propose a modal prompt with mandatory text (min 3 chars) before confirming soft removal.
